What service may include and what shapes the visit

A plumbing company may assess a reported issue, look at the affected plumbing, and explain a possible repair or other next step. Key facts:
- Coverage: Confirm the provider can assess the exact problem you’re reporting. A general description of plumbing service doesn’t confirm every task is offered.
