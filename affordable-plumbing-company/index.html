Scope, timing, and useful plumbing terms

Plumbing service can involve investigating a reported problem, explaining what may be causing it, and discussing possible work. The exact work depends on what the plumber finds and what you need. Confirm which tasks the company can handle when you call.

Key facts to check:
- What it covers: Ask what inspection or repair is included, and what would be treated as separate work.
- Typical timing: Timing depends on the issue, the work involved, and scheduling. Ask when the company can assess it and how long the proposed work may take.
- General cost factors: The problem's location, how much work is needed, parts, and access to the affected area can affect cost. Ask what is known before work starts and what could change the amount.
- Provider comparison: Compare the same details with each company: scope, timing, parts, and how changes are discussed. The lowest initial figure may not describe the same work.

You may hear diagnosis, meaning the process of finding the cause; scope, meaning the tasks included; and estimate, meaning an expected cost based on the information available. Ask the plumber to explain any term that isn't clear.
