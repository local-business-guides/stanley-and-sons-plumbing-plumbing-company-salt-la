What service involves and what to ask

The work starts with a description of the problem and may involve an inspection before a repair or other service is recommended. Timing varies with the issue, access to the affected area, the amount of work involved, and whether any needed parts are available. Cost also depends on factors such as the source of the problem, labor required, materials, and the scope of the repair. Ask for an explanation of those factors before deciding how to proceed.

For a quick comparison between providers, consider asking:
- What information do you need to understand my concern?
- Does the suggested work address the cause or only a visible symptom?
- What could change the expected timing or total scope?
- Will you explain the work before beginning?

A few terms may come up. A fixture is a plumbing-connected item such as a faucet or toilet. A drain carries wastewater away from a fixture. A shutoff valve controls water flow to a line or fixture. Knowing the words can help, but describing what you see is enough to begin.
