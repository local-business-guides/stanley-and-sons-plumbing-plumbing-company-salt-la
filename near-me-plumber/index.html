How to discuss the job and compare options

A plumber may need to learn more about the symptoms or inspect the affected area before explaining a repair. The timing can depend on the nature of the issue, access to the plumbing, the extent of work, and availability of any necessary parts. Cost is shaped by the cause, labor, materials, and the size of the job; there isn’t one figure that applies to every call. Ask what is included in the proposed work and what could change the scope.

Useful questions to ask include:
- What should I do before you arrive, if anything?
- What do you need to inspect to identify the problem?
- Which parts of the work are included in the proposed scope?
- What could affect the timing or cost once you assess it?

You may hear a few plumbing terms. A leak is water escaping from a pipe or connection. A clog is a blockage that restricts flow through a drain. A fixture is an installed item such as a sink faucet or toilet. You can simply describe the symptom if you’re unsure which term applies.
